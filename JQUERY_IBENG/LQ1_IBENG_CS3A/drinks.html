Basi (Sugarcane Wine) 750ml per bottle Basi is a traditional Ilocano fermented sugarcane wine with a slightly sweet and tangy flavor. Aged in clay jars, it develops a rich taste, making it a beloved drink for special occasions. It is best enjoyed as a cultural drink, often paired with local delicacies.
  • Sugarcane juice
  • yeast
  • bark extracts
Salabat (Ginger Tea) 250ml per bottle / 100g per pack (powdered form) Salabat is a soothing Filipino ginger tea known for its warming and healing properties. It is often consumed to relieve sore throats, colds, or as a daily wellness drink. This aromatic and slightly spicy beverage is best enjoyed hot with honey or calamansi.
  • Sugar or honey (optional)
  • Ginger
  • water
  • calamansi (optional)
Kalamansi Juice 350ml per bottle Kalamansi juice is a refreshing citrus drink made from freshly squeezed kalamansi, a small but flavorful Filipino lime. It is packed with Vitamin C and is known for its tangy yet sweet taste. This drink is a favorite thirst quencher, especially when served cold with ice.
  • Kalamansi extract
  • water
  • sugar or honey
  • butter
Sago’t Gulaman 500ml per bottle Sago’t Gulaman is a popular street drink made with sweet caramelized syrup, chewy tapioca pearls (sago), and jelly (gulaman). This refreshing beverage is known for its combination of textures and deep brown sugar flavor. It is a classic summer drink, perfect for cooling down on hot days.
  • Brown sugar
  • water
  • tapioca pearls
  • agar-agar (gulaman)
  • vanilla extract
Buko Juice (Coconut Water) 500ml per bottle / Fresh from coconut Buko juice is the natural, hydrating water from young coconuts, known for its light sweetness and refreshing taste. It is rich in electrolytes, making it a perfect drink for rehydration and energy replenishment. Often served fresh, it can also be mixed with coconut meat for added texture.
  • Fresh coconut water
  • coconut meat (optional)
  • sugar (optional)
Taho Drink 350ml per serving Taho is a warm, sweet drink made with soft silken tofu, arnibal (caramelized syrup), and chewy sago pearls. It is a classic Filipino street food drink enjoyed in the mornings. The combination of warm tofu and sweet syrup makes it a comforting and filling treat.
  • Silken tofu
  • brown sugar syrup
  • sago pearls
  • butter
  • vanilla extract
Guyabano Juice (Soursop Juice) 1350ml per bottle Guyabano juice is a tropical fruit drink known for its creamy texture and slightly tangy taste. It is packed with antioxidants and is believed to have various health benefits, including immune-boosting properties. This refreshing juice is perfect when served chilled or blended with ice.
  • Guyabano pulp
  • sugar or honey
  • water
Ube Shake 500ml per serving Ube shake is a rich and creamy drink made from purple yam (ube), milk, and crushed ice. It has a distinct sweet and nutty flavor with a vibrant purple color, making it visually appealing. This popular Filipino dessert drink is often topped with whipped cream or leche flan.
  • Ube puree
  • Sugar
  • milk (evaporated or condensed)
  • ice
Tablea Hot Chocolate (Tsokolate Eh) 250ml per cup Tsokolate Eh is a traditional Filipino hot chocolate made from pure cacao tablea, giving it a rich and bold chocolate taste. Often whisked to a frothy consistency, this drink is a favorite during breakfast or on cold rainy days. It is commonly served with traditional bread like pan de sal.
  • Cacao tablea
  • sugar
  • hot water or milk
Pandan Iced Tea 350ml per bottle Pandan iced tea is a refreshing herbal drink infused with the aromatic flavor of pandan leaves. This mildly sweet and fragrant tea is known for its calming and digestive benefits. It is often served cold with ice and a hint of lemon for extra zest.
  • Sugar
  • Pandan leaves
  • water
  • lemon (optional)
