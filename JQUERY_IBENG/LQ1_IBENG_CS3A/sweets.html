Chocnut 24g per pack Chocnut is a beloved Filipino peanut milk chocolate candy with a crumbly texture that melts in your mouth. It has a perfect balance of sweetness and nuttiness, making it a nostalgic treat for many Filipinos. Popular as a snack or a topping for desserts, Chocnut continues to be a favorite across generations.
  • roasted peanuts
  • Sugar
  • milk powder
  • cocoa powder
  • Fruit juice concentrate
  • vanilla
  • salt
Pastillas de Leche 100g per pack Pastillas de Leche are soft, milky confections made from carabao’s milk and sugar, offering a rich, creamy taste. These bite-sized treats are smooth and slightly chewy, making them a delightful snack or gift item. Traditionally handmade, they showcase the simplicity and quality of Filipino dairy-based sweets.
  • Sugar
  • cornstarch
  • Carabao’s milk
  • Vanilla
  • butter
Yema Balls 150g per pack Yema balls are sweet, custard-like candies with a rich, caramelized milk flavor and a soft, chewy texture. Often shaped into bite-sized balls and coated with sugar, they offer a satisfying sweetness that pairs well with coffee or tea. This traditional Filipino candy is made with egg yolks and condensed milk, cooked to perfection for a smooth and creamy finish.
  • Sugar
  • Condensed milk
  • egg yolks
  • butter
  • vanilla extract
Polvoron 120g per pack (assorted flavors) Polvoron is a crumbly, melt-in-your-mouth milk-based candy made with toasted flour, sugar, and butter. Available in various flavors like classic, ube, and pinipig, it is a favorite snack for Filipinos of all ages. This candy is often wrapped individually, making it a convenient and delicious treat for any occasion.
  • Sugar
  • Toasted flour
  • powdered milk
  • butter
  • pinipig (optional)
  • flavoring (e.g., ube, chocolate)
Butterscotch Bars 200g per pack Butterscotch bars are chewy, buttery, and caramel-like sweets infused with a hint of brown sugar and nuts. These soft yet slightly crisp treats are a popular delicacy, especially in the Visayas region. They are perfect as a snack or dessert, providing a rich and indulgent experience in every bite.
  • Brown Sugar
  • Glucose syrup
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Natural flavor
  • Food coloring
  • eggs
  • flour
  • nuts (optional)
Macapuno Balls 150g per pack Macapuno Balls are soft and chewy coconut-based candies made from preserved macapuno (a special type of coconut). These bite-sized sweets are naturally sweet and have a rich, creamy texture, making them a favorite Filipino delicacy. Often enjoyed as a snack or dessert, macapuno balls are perfect for those who love the tropical taste of coconut.
  • Macapuno coconut strips
  • sugar
  • condensed milk
  • butter
  • vanilla extract
Piyaya 180g per pack (5 pieces per pack) Piyaya is a flaky, sweet-filled flatbread candy with a caramelized muscovado sugar filling. Originating from Negros, this delicacy offers a satisfying crunch combined with a rich, syrupy sweetness. It is often enjoyed as a merienda (snack) and pairs well with coffee or tea.
  • wheat flour
  • muscovado sugar
  • margarine
  • water
  • sesame seeds
Sampalok Candy (Tamarind Candy) 100g per pack Sampalok Candy is a tangy and sweet tamarind-based treat coated in sugar for an irresistible balance of flavors. It has a chewy texture with a hint of sourness, making it a refreshing and addictive snack. Some varieties include a spicy kick for those who love a little heat in their sweets.
  • Tamarind pulp
  • Sugar
  • salt
  • chili powder
Bukayo 120g per pack Bukayo is a traditional Filipino coconut candy made from caramelized shredded coconut. It has a chewy, sticky texture and is known for its deep, rich sweetness, perfect for satisfying sugar cravings. Bukayo is often used as a topping for kakanin (Filipino rice cakes) or eaten on its own as a delicious snack.
  • Grated coconut
  • brown sugar
  • coconut milk
  • vanilla extract
Ube Pastillas 100g per pack Ube Pastillas are a delightful twist on the classic pastillas de leche, infused with the rich, earthy flavor of ube (purple yam). These soft, creamy, and mildly sweet treats are a must-try for ube lovers. With their vibrant purple hue and smooth texture, they make for a visually appealing and delicious snack.
  • Sugar
  • Carabao’s milk
  • Butter
  • ube extract
  • cornstarch
