Dragon Fruit (Pitaya) 300-600g per fruit Dragon fruit, locally grown in Ilocos, is known for its vibrant pink skin and white or red flesh speckled with tiny black seeds. It has a mildly sweet, refreshing taste and is often eaten fresh, blended into smoothies, or used in desserts. This superfruit is packed with fiber and essential nutrients.

Native Guava (Bayabas) 200-500g per fruit Guavas are small, round fruits with green or yellow skin and soft pink or white flesh. They have a sweet and slightly tart flavor and can be eaten raw, juiced, or made into jams. Known for their high Vitamin C content, guavas boost immunity and digestion.

Pomelo (Suha) 1-2 kg per fruit Pomelo is a large citrus fruit with a thick rind and juicy, mildly sweet flesh. It is commonly eaten fresh or made into fruit salads and juices. Pomelo is an excellent source of Vitamin C and is known to aid digestion and hydration.

  • fiber
Star Apple (Caimito) 250-500g per fruit Star apple is a round fruit with purple or green skin and creamy, sweet, jelly-like flesh. It is often eaten fresh, chilled, or added to fruit salads. Its rich fiber content makes it beneficial for digestion and overall health.

Talong(Eggplant) 250g per bundle Eggplant is a staple vegetable in Ilocano cuisine, commonly used in dishes like pinakbet and tortang talong (eggplant omelet). It has a mild, slightly sweet taste and a soft texture when cooked. Eggplants are rich in fiber and essential nutrients that support heart health.

Ampalaya (Bitter Gourd ) 200-300g per piece Ampalaya, or bitter gourd, is known for its distinct bitter taste and high nutritional value. It is often stir-fried, used in soups, or cooked with eggs for a nutritious meal. This vegetable is well-known for helping regulate blood sugar levels.

Malunggay (Moringa Leaves) 100g per bundle Malunggay, or moringa, is a nutrient-rich leafy vegetable often used in soups, stews, and herbal teas. It is known as a "superfood" due to its numerous health benefits, including immune support and anti-inflammatory properties. This vegetable is a staple ingredient in Ilocano dishes like dinengdeng.

Okra (Lady’s Finger) 250g per pack Okra is a green, pod-shaped vegetable that becomes soft and slightly slimy when cooked. It is commonly used in pinakbet and other Ilocano dishes. Rich in fiber, okra aids digestion and helps control blood sugar levels.

Kalabasa(Squash) 500g-1kg per piece Squash is a versatile vegetable commonly used in Filipino dishes like ginataang kalabasa and soups. It has a mildly sweet taste and a soft texture when cooked. This nutrient-dense vegetable is great for improving eye health and boosting immunity.
